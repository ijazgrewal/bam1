Frequently Addressed Questions

Everything you need to know about scheduling, changes, and on-ground logistics.

flight_land What occurs if our flight into Jeddah or Madinah is delayed?

expand_more

We actively track your inbound flight through live air-traffic radar systems using your provided flight number. Whether your flight is delayed by 30 minutes or 4 hours, our chauffeur schedules arrival based on wheels-down time. We include a complimentary 90-minute grace waiting period following international touchdown, giving your family ample time to clear immigration, collect baggage, and use amenities.

checkroom Can our chauffeur stop at the Meeqat so we can assume Ihram?

expand_more

Yes. If you are traveling from Madinah toward Makkah, we routinely stop at Meeqat Dhu'l-Hulayfah (Masjid ash-Shajarah). When traveling from Taif, we halt at Qarn al-Manazil (As-Sayl Al-Kabir). Chauffeurs provide sufficient time for ghusl/wudu, changing into Ihram garments, and making the intention with two rak'ahs of salah without rushing.

payments What payment methods are supported?

expand_more

We provide full payment flexibility. You may prepay securely online using Visa, Mastercard, American Express, or Apple Pay. For enterprise and agency partners, international bank transfers (SWIFT/IBAN) with official VAT tax invoices are provided. Alternatively, cash settlement in Saudi Riyals (SAR) upon vehicle arrival can be coordinated with your dispatcher.

speed How far in advance should we reserve, and do you handle last-minute requests?

expand_more

We recommend booking at least 48 to 72 hours prior to arrival, particularly during peak pilgrimage periods (Rajab, Sha'ban, Ramadan, and the Dhul Hijjah season). However, our dedicated 24/7 central dispatch desk maintains dedicated staging vehicles and can accommodate urgent dispatch requests within 60 to 90 minutes in Jeddah and Makkah.
